About Us

What we are

The Downtown Manteca Improvement Association is the business association for the blocks around Yosemite and Maple. Four officers, an executive director, a board and three standing committees, meeting at the American Legion Hall three Wednesdays a month.

What we work on

The events are the visible part — the Cinco de Mayo car show, the Fourth of July parade, the Beer & Wine Stroll. The rest of the year goes on the things nobody photographs: business development, beautification, placemaking and the long-term case for investing in these blocks. Most of it happens in partnership with the City of Manteca, local organizations and the businesses themselves.

Who shows up

Business owners, property owners, civic leaders and volunteers. You do not need to own a business to come, and you do not need to be on the agenda to speak. Agendas go up before each meeting and minutes go up after; both live on this site, along with public notices and a form for records requests.

Get Involved

For information on upcoming meetings, events, and opportunities to get involved, please visit our Events Calendar or contact our team at info@downtownmanteca.com.

All meetings are open to the public, and community participation is encouraged.
