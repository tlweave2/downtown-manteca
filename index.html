What the District Does

Work that happens between the events.

The Downtown Manteca District represents the businesses and property owners along West Yosemite Avenue and the blocks around it. The work splits about evenly between things you can see — beautification, placemaking, events that give people a reason to park and walk — and things you mostly don't, like advocating with the City of Manteca on sidewalks, storefronts, and long-term investment downtown.

The District is run by a board of business owners, property owners, and residents, with committees that meet through the month. None of it is closed-door. If you do business downtown, or you just live here and have opinions about it, the room is open.
